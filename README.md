🛒 Online Shopping Customer Behaviour Analysis
Tableau Final Project
📊 Project Overview

The Online Shopping Customer Behaviour Analysis project uses Tableau to analyze customer purchasing patterns from an e-commerce dataset. The dashboard provides insights into product categories, color preferences, customer demographics, purchase history, review ratings, and spending behavior, helping businesses make data-driven decisions.

📝 Abstract

The rapid growth of e-commerce platforms has generated large volumes of customer data that can be leveraged to understand purchasing behavior and improve business strategies. This project focuses on building an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze customer demographics, product categories, color preferences, purchase history, review ratings, and spending patterns. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable insights, enabling businesses to optimize product offerings, enhance customer experience, and improve sales performance.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze customer purchasing behavior across product categories

Identify popular product categories and color preferences

Understand the relationship between age, previous purchases, and spending

Analyze customer satisfaction using review ratings

Build an interactive dashboard for data-driven business decisions

📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer shopping behavior data collected from an online retail platform, including demographic details and purchase history.

Key Attributes

Customer ID – Unique identifier for customers

Category – Product category (Accessories, Clothing, Footwear, Outerwear)

Color – Product color preference

Age – Customer age

Previous Purchases – Number of past purchases

Review Rating – Customer rating

Purchase Amount (USD) – Amount spent per purchase

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Tableau Desktop – Dashboard development and visualization

Microsoft Excel / CSV – Dataset storage and preprocessing

Data Analytics Techniques – Aggregation, comparison, and trend analysis

📈 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is designed with a clear and user-friendly layout, combining KPI indicators with detailed visual insights.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Average Previous Purchases: 4.00

Average Review Rating: 4.80

Average Purchase Amount: 31.00 USD

Average Customer Age: 57 years

🔍 Key Insights

Clothing is the most popular category across all age groups

Customers with higher previous purchases tend to spend more

Neutral colors like Black, Gray, and Blue are most preferred

High review ratings (4.8 avg) indicate strong customer satisfaction

Repeat customers contribute significantly to overall revenue

📌 Business Impact

Helps identify high-performing product categories

Supports inventory planning and promotional strategies

Guides product design using color preference analysis

Improves customer retention through loyalty insights

✅ Conclusion

This project demonstrates the effective use of Tableau to analyze and visualize customer behavior data. The dashboard delivers meaningful insights into purchasing trends, customer preferences, and satisfaction levels, supporting informed decisions in product strategy, marketing, and customer engagement.
